# Notification Icon Tray

An Android-style notification shade with an icon tray pinned at the bottom. As notifications scroll under the tray they collapse into small icons that slide in from the left and join the group. Everything is a continuous function of scroll position, so fast drags are fast, slow drags are slow, and it plays backwards when you drag the other way. All apps, names and messages in the demo are fictional.

- `AndroidStyleNotificationIconTray.swift`: the interaction, unchanged from the original file.
- `Usage.swift`: how to add it to an app target (iOS 17+).
