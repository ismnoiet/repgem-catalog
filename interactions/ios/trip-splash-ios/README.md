# Trip Splash

Your logo appears and an orange dot eats its letters one by one. The dot then stretches into a streak that zips across the screen, and a big circle sweeps in from the left to open up your first screen. Hand it your own screen to show, or leave it empty to see a plain placeholder. The circle only uncovers your screen instead of redrawing it, so it stays light on the phone. The placeholder screen in the demo is neutral and has no real content.

- `TripSplashAnimation.swift`: the interaction. `TripSplashView { YourScreen() }` reveals your own screen (iOS 17+).
- `Usage.swift`: how to add it to an app target.
- `Demo.swift`, `DemoScreen.swift`: preview inside the shared demo frame. Not needed in your app.

Inspired by the launch animation of the Trip.com app; the code is original.
